## For the business owner

You already fund AI coding tools. The leak is not the seat price — it is **rework, wrong-doc sessions, and quality bars that loosen under deadline**. More seats, a rules dump, or staff-aug with nothing left in git do not close that leak ([../competition/landscape.md](../competition/landscape.md)).

You buy a **bounded, priced change project**: expert judgment on your repos, plus a governing framework that stays in **your** git after we leave. Done means foundation and a real working-loop win with proof when behavior moves — not a promised productivity %. Price and duration are set after qualify ([offer-model.md](offer-model.md#you--we--exit)). Tool-cost context: [why-railkit.md](why-railkit.md#status-quo-cost-illustrative).

## Value and purpose

Plain words for both sides of the deal. Mechanics and exits: [offer-model.md](offer-model.md#you--we--exit).

| Part | Purpose | Value for the **buyer** | Value for the **seller** |
|------|---------|-------------------------|--------------------------|
| **Change project** (what you pay for) | A human expert with broad domain expertise, working with the kit, brings one (or few) repos up to speed in a fixed order | You get judgment on *your* mess — owners chosen, dual docs closed, checks baselined, a real product seam fixed — not a slide deck | You sell a bounded, productized engagement with a clear done-bar; you do not trap value only in chat hours |
| **Governing framework / kit** (what you keep) | In-repo rules of work so every editor (person or agent) finds the deciding doc, proves behavior changes, and cannot quietly loosen quality bars | After we leave, the rules stay in **your** git; AI tools and new hires stop paying situating tax on the same chaos | Proof the offer is transferable; not staff-aug theater; claims match shipped `kit/` |

**Together:** the engagement installs and proves the shape; the kit is why the shape survives. Neither alone is the product. **Instead of:** more AI-editor seats, a Cursor rules pack, wiki playbooks, or staff-aug with no in-repo artifact ([../competition/landscape.md](../competition/landscape.md)).

### Not this product (say in the first minute)

We are **not** selling Cursor, Copilot, or model seats. We are **not** a Cursor rules pack, staff-aug without leaving process in git, or an “AI operating system.” We sell a **priced change project** (expert + kit). Category detail: [usp-moat.md](usp-moat.md#category). Wedges vs alternatives: [../competition/landscape.md](../competition/landscape.md).

### Privacy (one line, then stop)

Installing the kit adds files to **your** git. Agent/model vendors are a **separate egress** (prompts and selected files may leave your network). We name that; we do not claim Railkit makes you GDPR/AI-Act compliant. Detail: [../consulting/privacy-and-legality.md](../consulting/privacy-and-legality.md).

## 60-second pitch

You already pay for AI coding tools. The waste is not the seat price — it is sessions spent on the wrong docs, rework, and quality bars that quietly loosen. **Instead of** buying more seats, dropping a rules pack, or hiring staff-aug with nothing left in git, we sell a **priced transformation**: a human expert with broad domain expertise, paired with our installable kit, brings one (or few) repos up to speed in a fixed order and **leaves the governing framework in your git**. You keep your editors. You keep the kit. You must bring a named decider, repo and CI access, and a live delivery seam. Success is foundation plus a real on-contact win with proof when behavior moves — not a promised productivity multiplier. Price is set per engagement.

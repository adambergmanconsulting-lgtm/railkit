## One-paragraph offer

Railkit is a **change project** that brings your codebase up to speed in a fixed order, plus a **governing framework** left in the repo so every editor (person or agent) keeps improving coherence, efficiency, clarity, and quality. First: named document owners and file-size **ratchets** (budgets that only tighten). Next: the working loop. Then: ideal merge gates (including Knip-class unused exports) shipped or on a **dated queue** (named owner + due date). Agents auto-run must/should fixes; humans decide Prefer/Avoid forks. You keep the kit after the engagement.
